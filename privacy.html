Privacy notice

In short

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no fonts or scripts from third parties. The only personal data it handles is what you choose to send through the join and contact forms.

Details

Responsible party

West Point Society of Switzerland, Switzerland. Reach the society through the contact form. Data protection contact: Christian Pierce, founding coordinator, West Point Society of Switzerland.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (San Francisco, USA). When you visit, Netlify's servers process technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, as any web server does, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. Netlify's own privacy notice describes its handling of that data.

Forms

If you use the join or contact form, the details you enter are transmitted to Netlify, stored in the society's Netlify account, and forwarded by email to the society's leadership. We use them only to run the society: to keep a membership list and to contact you about events and society business. We do not sell or share them with third parties. Membership data is kept for as long as you are a member; contact-form messages are deleted once dealt with.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and where applicable the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you may ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent to further contact at any time. Write to the address above.

External links

Links to the West Point Association of Graduates, Sallyport, and other sites lead to services with their own privacy practices.
