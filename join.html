Join the society

Who can join

Membership categories follow the framework of the West Point Association of Graduates.

  • Graduates of the United States Military Academy at West Point living in Switzerland or nearby.
  • Former cadets who attended the Academy.
  • Spouses, widows, and widowers of graduates.
  • Friends of West Point: parents of cadets, former faculty and staff, and others with a close connection to the Academy.

Graduates of the other U.S. service academies are welcome as guests at most events and are invited to join our shared gatherings.

What membership involves

Dues
None at present.
Commitment
Come when you can. Volunteer if you like.
Communication
Occasional emails about events and society business. No newsletters you did not ask for.

Please also update your address with WPAOG. The Association assigns graduates to societies by residence, and an accurate record is what makes Switzerland count.
